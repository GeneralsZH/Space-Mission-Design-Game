Space Mission Architect: Trade-Off
An educational engineering simulation for designing, assembling, and launching a lunar satellite right in your browser.
An educational spacecraft engineering simulation — design, build, launch, and operate.

🎯 Project Goal
To create a visual, interactive environment that allows users to learn the fundamentals of spacecraft systems engineering in a gamified format and understand key mission design trade-offs—balancing scientific value, mass, power consumption, reliability, and budget.

🚀 About the Project
Space Mission Architect is a browser-based educational lab where the user goes through the complete lifecycle of creating a space mission:

📋 Contract Review — Setting the objectives for the Lunar Ice Mapper mission to map water ice in the Moon's polar craters.
🛒 Spacecraft Design & Assembly — Selecting components from a catalog (buses, instruments, antennas, solar panels, batteries, thrusters, shielding) and assembling them in 3D.
🛸 Orbit & Launch Vehicle Selection — Calculating delta-V (

), power budget, and total spacecraft mass.
🧪 Pre-Launch Testing — Verifying systems on test benches (power, thermal vacuum, communications, vibration) to increase overall mission reliability.
📡 Mission Control — Monitoring the launch and orbital flight in 3D, tracking telemetry, and making decisions during anomalies (solar flares, eclipses).
🏆 Mission Evaluation — Calculating the final mission score based on the volume of scientific data gathered, reliability, and budget efficiency.
Note: All physical and economic parameters in the simulator are educational approximations and are not intended for calculating real-world space missions.
